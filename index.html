Scroll & Stitch

Turn a screen recording or screenshots into one long screenshot.

For best results

  1. Scroll slowly and pause on each screen.
  2. Avoid tapping the page while recording.
  3. Start and end on the page you’re capturing.
  4. With screenshots, overlap each one with the last.
